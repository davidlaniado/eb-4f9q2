Limitations, honest

  • No impressions or spend for US commercial ads. Copies and days running are the ceiling. "Sort by impressions" only does something on EU and political ads.
  • 150 ads per run. This brand has about 1,300. Pulling all of them is about $4.50 on the official scraper, or $0.40 on the cheap one.
  • Monthly visits are unreliable for burner-domain brands. Needs a SimilarWeb pull.
  • Frame sheets are 9 frames, not full watches. Transcripts cost extra (memo23 option) or come from TrendTrack.
  • They can say what we can't. Organs, med names, "cure," fake doctors. We copy the container and the angle, never the claim.
  • Catalog ads show up as {{product.brand}}; ignore them.
